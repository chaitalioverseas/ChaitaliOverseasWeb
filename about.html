Who We Are

Chaitali Overseas – Connecting Indian Agricultural Products with Global Buyers

Chaitali Overseas is an India-based merchant exporter focused on supplying quality agricultural products to international buyers. We specialize in premium Indian rice and are expanding our product portfolio to include spices, cereals, pulses and other agricultural commodities.

Our approach is built around dependable product sourcing, consistent quality, export-ready packaging, competitive pricing and professional communication. We work to understand each buyer's product specifications, quantity, packaging and destination requirements before arranging the appropriate supply and export process.

We aim to build long-term business relationships with importers, wholesalers, distributors, retailers and food businesses looking for reliable suppliers of Indian agricultural products.

Global Market Focus

Supporting international buyers with Indian agricultural products and export-oriented supply solutions.

Quality-Focused Sourcing
