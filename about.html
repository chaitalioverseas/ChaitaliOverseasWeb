WHO WE ARE

Connecting International Buyers with Indian Agricultural Products

Chaitali Overseas is an India-based merchant export business focused on supplying quality agricultural products from India to international markets. Our current product portfolio includes Indian rice, makhana and jute, with an expanding range of cereals, spices, pulses and other agricultural commodities.

As a merchant exporter from India, we work with reliable suppliers and sourcing networks to coordinate product requirements according to the needs of overseas buyers. We focus on product specifications, quality requirements, packaging, export documentation and shipment coordination to support smooth and professional international trade.

We aim to build dependable, transparent and long-term business relationships with importers, wholesalers, distributors, trading companies and bulk buyers looking to source agricultural products from India.

Global Market Focus

We support international buyers looking to source selected Indian agricultural products for their respective markets, with sourcing and export arrangements based on product availability, specifications, quantity and destination requirements.

Quality-Focused Sourcing

We coordinate with reliable Indian suppliers and focus on buyer-specific product specifications, quality requirements and packaging expectations before proceeding with export orders.

WHAT WE DO

Agricultural Export Solutions from India

Chaitali Overseas provides sourcing and export coordination for buyers seeking agricultural products from India. Our services are structured around the requirements of each product, buyer and destination market.

Indian Rice Export

Our current product focus includes Indian rice, including Basmati and Non-Basmati varieties according to buyer requirements. We coordinate product specifications, quantities, packaging and export arrangements for international buyers.

Indian Makhana

We supply Indian makhana, also known as fox nuts or lotus seeds, for international markets. Product grade, quantity, packaging and specifications can be coordinated according to buyer requirements and destination market needs.

Indian Jute Products

We source selected Indian jute products according to buyer requirements, product specifications and quantity needs, with export coordination and shipment support for international markets.

Indian Spices

Our expanding product portfolio includes selected Indian spices such as turmeric, cumin, chilli, coriander and other spices, subject to product availability and buyer requirements.

Indian Cereals & Grains

We source selected Indian cereals and grain-based agricultural commodities for bulk and commercial requirements, with specifications and packaging arranged according to buyer needs.

Indian Pulses & Legumes

Our agricultural product portfolio is expanding to include Indian pulses and legumes such as chickpeas, lentils, green gram and other products according to market demand and sourcing availability.

Product Sourcing

Export Coordination
