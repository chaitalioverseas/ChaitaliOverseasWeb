Our Export Process

How We Handle an Export Inquiry

Every international order can have different product, quantity, packaging, documentation and destination requirements. Our export process is therefore coordinated around the specific requirements of each buyer and order.

01

Buyer Inquiry

The process begins when an importer, wholesaler, distributor or retailer shares their requirement with us.

02

Requirement Discussion

We discuss product type, variety, quantity, specifications, packaging, destination and other relevant requirements.

03

Product Sourcing

Suitable sourcing arrangements are coordinated based on the agreed product requirements and commercial terms.

04

Specification & Order Confirmation

Product specifications, quantity, packaging, pricing and applicable commercial terms are confirmed before proceeding with the order.

05

Packaging & Export Preparation

Products are prepared and packaged according to the agreed requirements and applicable shipment conditions.

06

Export Documentation

Required export and shipment documentation is coordinated according to the product, destination and applicable requirements.

07

Logistics & Shipment Coordination

Shipment arrangements are coordinated with the relevant logistics and shipping partners according to the agreed terms.

08

Shipment Dispatch

Once the shipment is ready and relevant arrangements are completed, the order proceeds toward international dispatch.

09

Shipment Documentation & Follow-Up

Relevant shipment information and documentation are shared and followed up according to the agreed transaction and shipping arrangements.

Export Process FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I start an export inquiry with Chaitali Overseas?

You can contact us with your required product, quantity, destination country, packaging preference and other specifications. Our team can then discuss the available sourcing and export options.

What agricultural products do you export?

Our current focus is Indian rice. We are also developing our portfolio of spices, cereals, pulses and other agricultural products for international markets.

Can product specifications be customized?

Product specifications and packaging requirements can be discussed according to the product, destination and buyer's requirements before confirming an order.

Do export requirements vary by country?

Yes. Product, documentation, packaging, import and other requirements can vary depending on the commodity and destination market. Requirements are therefore considered for each shipment.
