Our Export Process

How We Handle an Export Inquiry

Every international order can involve different product, quality, packaging, documentation and destination requirements. Our export process is structured to understand these requirements and coordinate suitable sourcing and export arrangements for each buyer and order.

01

Buyer Inquiry

We receive enquiries from importers, wholesalers, distributors and other international buyers with specific product requirements.

02

Requirement Discussion

We discuss product type, variety, quality, quantity, packaging, destination and other applicable requirements with the buyer.

03

Product Sourcing

We coordinate with suitable Indian suppliers to identify products that match the agreed specifications and commercial requirements.

04

Specification & Order Confirmation

Product specifications, quantity, packaging and applicable commercial requirements are reviewed and confirmed before order processing.

05

Packaging & Export Preparation

Packaging requirements are coordinated according to product type, quantity, buyer preferences and destination-market requirements.

06

Export Documentation

Required export and shipment documentation is coordinated according to the product, destination and agreed trade requirements.

07

Logistics & Shipment Coordination

We coordinate relevant logistics arrangements and shipment requirements with appropriate partners according to the agreed terms.

08

Shipment Dispatch

Once the shipment is ready and required arrangements are completed, the goods are dispatched according to the agreed export schedule.

09

Shipment Documentation & Follow-Up

Relevant shipment documentation is shared and follow-up is coordinated according to the agreed export and logistics arrangements.

Export Process FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Agricultural Exports from India

How can I start an export enquiry with Chaitali Overseas?

You can contact Chaitali Overseas by sharing your required product, quantity, specifications, packaging requirements, destination country or port and any other relevant requirements. We review the enquiry and coordinate suitable sourcing and export options.

What agricultural products do you export?

Chaitali Overseas currently focuses on Indian rice, makhana and jute. Our expanding portfolio also includes selected spices, cereals, pulses and other agricultural commodities according to buyer requirements and sourcing availability.

Can product specifications be customized?

Yes. Product specifications such as variety, grade, quantity, packaging and other applicable requirements can be discussed according to the product and buyer's requirements before order confirmation.

Do you export agricultural products to different countries?

Chaitali Overseas serves international buyers and coordinates export requirements according to the destination country, product specifications, quantity, packaging and agreed commercial terms.

Can I request a quotation for bulk agricultural products?

Yes. International importers, wholesalers, distributors and bulk buyers can submit their product and quantity requirements to request an export quotation.

How is agricultural product packaging arranged for export?

Packaging requirements are discussed according to the product type, order quantity, buyer preference and destination-market requirements before the export order is processed.
