About Us

Leading Merchant Exporter of Premium Indian Rice & Agricultural Products

Chaitali Overseas is an India-based merchant exporter committed to supplying high-quality agricultural products to customers worldwide. We specialize in Premium Basmati Rice, Non-Basmati Rice and are expanding our product portfolio to include cereals, spices, pulses, and other farm-fresh agricultural commodities. Our focus is on delivering consistent quality, competitive pricing, secure packaging, and timely shipments while building long-term relationships with global importers, wholesalers, distributors, and retailers.
